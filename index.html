the story so far

One bedroom studio.
One big dream.

OkieMookie started as a love letter to K-pop — late nights on BandLab chasing the perfect drop, and a growing stash of photocards too good not to share. What began as a personal obsession is turning into a full creative home.

Today that means production lessons and a photocard shop. Tomorrow it's a full Shopify storefront and a Skool community where producers and collectors grow together. You're getting in on the ground floor.

100% self-taught & DIY
